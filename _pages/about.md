# Lin Li

Hi! 👋

I am a Master's student in **[Psychology: Data Science in Human Behavior](https://datascience.psych.wisc.edu/)** at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://www.wisc.edu). At the core of my journey is a continuous fascination with **people**-specifically, how we interact with our environments and how data-driven systems can be built to better serve human needs.

My background bridges built environments, renewable energy, and data science. Prior to UW-Madison, I completed my Master's in Building Technology Science at **[Xi'an University of Architecture and Technology](https://www.xauat.edu.cn/gwyw/)** and worked as a Photovoltaic System Engineer at **[LONGI & CENTER Renewable Energy](https://en-bipv.centerint.com/)**. Across these roles, my work centered on sustainability and human-centric design-from green architectural environments to large-scale solar energy analytics. Working with real-world energy performance data inspired me to pursue UW-Madison, bridging my background with advanced data analytics and quantitative behavioral modeling.

Outside of data and analytics, I am currently picking up tennis and swimming. I also love traveling and capturing natural landscapes through photography.
